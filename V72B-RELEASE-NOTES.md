# V72B — Big Bad Barber Showcase

## Core game loop
- Beard Builder crates upgrade the persistent fortress above their reel.
- Five fortress positions progress through Stubble Shack, Lumber Beard Cabin, Viking Beard Hall, and Golden Beard Castle.
- Fortress levels persist between paid spins.
- A losing spin with no upgrade can summon the Big Bad Barber.
- The Barber visibly enters, targets a built fortress, shaves it, and reveals the stored multiplier.
- The attacked fortress resets after paying.

## Shave Down bonus
- 3+ Golden Razors trigger 8 free spins.
- Builders remain active during free spins.
- Failed build spins can trigger Barber attacks.
- Two early Razors trigger final-reel anticipation.

## Cabinet and controls
- New premium inline SVG symbols replace emoji placeholders.
- Shared V72A top-to-bottom reel engine retained.
- Correct Credit, Denomination, Bet, Win, Max Bet, Auto, and Spin controls.
- Auto options: 10, 25, 50, or unlimited; Spin changes to Stop while active.
- No full-screen disconnected bonus board and no black result gap.
