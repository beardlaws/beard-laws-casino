# V69 — Feature Moments & The Big Bad Barber

## Megh's Cosmic Jam
- UFO beam now selects visible tiles, abducts them into the craft, and drops replacements into only those positions.
- Alien Encore invasion now changes the board instead of playing a disconnected overlay.
- Goat Stampede prefers strawberry targets, runs across the screen, eats a random set of tiles, and respins only those cells.
- Other Cosmic Weather events visibly mutate only the affected positions.
- New replacement symbols fall into place instead of the entire grid flashing to a new result.

## The Big Bad Barber
- New playable 5×3, 243-ways slot added to the casino floor.
- Modern shared DOM reel animation with staggered stops.
- Golden Razors trigger The Shave Down.
- The barber attacks persistent Beard Fortresses.
- Surviving fortresses upgrade through larger multiplier levels.
- No Shave Wild substitutes on standard wins.
- Original Beard Laws theme and presentation, informed by the build/upgrade/reveal rhythm players enjoy in construction-feature slots without copying another game's art or exact rules.
