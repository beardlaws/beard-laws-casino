# Beard Laws Casino V53

V53 is the Bonus Revolution release. It preserves the verified wager engines while giving all three flagship slots a distinct, visible signature feature.

## Neema's Frozen Happy Hour

- Three Tickets or a full Departure meter launch a 5x4 hold-and-respin drink board before the voyage.
- Six drinks begin locked and every new drink resets three respins.
- Vodka collects, Ice doubles, Cranberry doubles a reel, Bartender Neema adds a drink, Last Call adds a respin, and Ship Wheel upgrades small values.
- Mini drinks can land and filling all 20 positions awards the 500x Grand.
- Frozen Happy Hour saves its live board, respins, and award locally until collected.
- Captain Neema's route choice, cabin upgrades, Party Cove, Ocean View, ports, retriggers, and Last Call finale remain intact.

## Megh's Cosmic Soundboard

- Cascades charge Bass, Lead, Drums, Vocals, and UFO channels above the reels.
- Three channels can launch Encore; all five launch Headliner Mode.
- Headliner begins at 5x with five extra drops, a maximum stage jump, and persistent multiplier-tile buildup.
- Existing Long Set, Power Chords, UFO Storm, retriggers, stage upgrades, and Guitar Smash remain.

## Beard Bank Heist 2.0

- Players choose Power Drill, Coin Magnet, or Skeleton Key before opening vault boxes.
- Drill can double cash boxes, Magnet adds credits to good picks, and Skeleton Key converts the first alarm into a win.
- Living Vault respins can trigger Shake the Vault upgrades and Break the Bank collections.
- The full-vault Grand, jackpot coins, Vernon Free Spins, 243-way wins, and 30-charge Living Vault remain.

Verification: TypeScript, production build, 16 tests, million-spin Beard Bank regression, and clean archive integrity.
