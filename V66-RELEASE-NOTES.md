# V66 — Real Reel Motion

## Rebuilt spin foundation
- Neema's High Seas now uses independent vertical reel strips instead of replacing the entire grid every frame.
- Beard Bank now accelerates, cruises, decelerates, overshoots, bounces, and locks reel by reel.
- Megh's Cosmic Jam now uses the same smooth reel-strip foundation before its cascade feature begins.
- Stopping one reel no longer rebuilds or glitches the remaining reels.
- Final outcomes are placed into each strip before the animation stops, eliminating the visible last-frame symbol swap.

## Timing and feel
- Base-game spins are deliberately slower and heavier.
- Auto play uses the complete reel animation rather than a shortened symbol shuffle.
- Scatter/ticket anticipation can extend the final reel without affecting earlier stopped reels.
- Beard Bank Vernon free spins now animate instead of instantly swapping results.

## Cosmic tumbles
- New symbols fall from above with column/row staggering and a soft landing instead of instantly appearing.
- Cascade timing was lengthened so wins, removal, and refill are readable.

## Neema symbol polish
- Chocolate milk art is larger in its tile.
- Its label has a higher-contrast backing and responsive sizing for desktop and mobile.

## Concept decision
The barber attacking beard buildings belongs in a separate future slot rather than being forced into Beard Bank. Working concept: **The Big Bad Barber**.
